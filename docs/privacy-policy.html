Privacy Policy

App: Seek and Catch  ·  Developer: Maxot  ·  Last updated: April 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Seek and Catch ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the application. We are committed to collecting only what is necessary and never selling your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Anonymous User ID. On first launch the App signs you in anonymously via Firebase Authentication. This generates a random identifier that is not linked to your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information.
  • Display Name. You may optionally choose a display name inside the App. This name is stored locally on your device and, if you appear on the leaderboard, it is stored in our database alongside your anonymous user ID and score.
  • Game Scores. Your best scores, selected game mode, and difficulty level are stored in Firebase Firestore to power the global leaderboard.
  • App Preferences. Settings such as theme (dark/light), colorblind mode, sound, and music preferences are stored only on your device using Android DataStore and are never sent to our servers.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Device location
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Contacts or files on your device
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Payment information

3. How We Use the Information

  • To display your score on the global leaderboard
  • To persist your preferences across sessions
  • To maintain a consistent anonymous identity so your leaderboard record is preserved

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the App's features.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following Firebase services provided by Google LLC:

  • Firebase Authentication – anonymous sign-in only
  • Firebase Firestore – cloud storage for leaderboard and user records

Google's privacy policy applies to Firebase data processing: https://policies.google.com/privacy

No advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or other third-party services are included in the App.

5. Data Retention

Your anonymous user ID and leaderboard records are retained as long as they are relevant to the leaderboard. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us (see Section 7). App preferences stored locally are deleted when you uninstall the App.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Because all authentication is fully anonymous (no name, email, or contact details are required), the App does not collect personal data from any user regardless of age.

7. Your Rights and Contact

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
